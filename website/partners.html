Are you in Galicia?

We bring software teams from northern Europe to the Galician countryside for five working days at a time, and we need people on the ground.

A team is 8 to 14 people. They arrive Monday morning, leave Friday evening, and want the whole property to themselves. We’re always looking for houses, cooks and people who know the region. If you own a property, cook, or run something we should know about — get in touch.

We’re running our first weeks in 2027, so we’re building the network now.

Who we’re looking for

Property owners

A house that sleeps 8 to 14 people in private rooms, with a room that can be a work room, real fibre internet, and exclusive use for the week. Inland, rural, and quiet is exactly right — a village of thirty people is a feature, not a problem.

Cooks

Local cooks who can feed a group properly for four days with local food. Also pulpeiros, churrasco, village bakeries — anyone who cooks the real thing.

Guides and wineries

One afternoon a week, everyone stops. Wineries in the Ribeira Sacra and the Rías Baixas, river trips, walks, and anyone who can show a group something worth seeing.

Drivers

Transfers from Porto and Santiago de Compostela on Monday mornings and Friday afternoons, for groups with luggage.
